Whisper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026
Version: 2026-03-v1

This Privacy Policy explains how Whisper collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you access or use the Whisper professional opportunity network.

1. Information You Provide

We collect information that you provide directly when creating or using a Whisper account. This may include:

  • name and contact information
  • professional credentials and license information
  • brokerage affiliation
  • phone number and email address
  • saved collections and notification preferences
  • information submitted when sharing an opportunity

2. Opportunity Information

Users may submit information describing potential real estate opportunities. This information may include property characteristics, approximate location, pricing ranges, status information, descriptions, and related professional notes.

Opportunity information is submitted by users and may be preliminary, incomplete, or subject to change.

3. Verification and Access Data

We collect and store information used to verify professional eligibility and control access to the platform. This may include:

  • license verification data
  • identity verification results
  • access request and onboarding records
  • approval, waitlist, or review status
  • signup continuation records

4. Authentication and Security Logs

When you use Whisper we may record authentication and security events, including:

  • sign-in link requests
  • QR sign-in events
  • token usage and expiration
  • login timestamps
  • session identifiers

These logs help protect accounts, detect misuse, and maintain system integrity.

5. Device and Log Data

We may collect certain technical information when you access Whisper, including:

  • IP address
  • browser type
  • user agent strings
  • device information
  • request logs
  • diagnostic and performance data

6. Legal Acceptance and Certification Records

We store records of Terms of Use and Privacy Policy acceptance. These records may include:

  • the legal version accepted
  • acceptance timestamps
  • associated IP address
  • user agent information

We also store certifications associated with opportunity sharing, including seller-direction confirmations, compliance acknowledgments, and information-accuracy certifications.

These records help maintain audit history and support platform integrity.

7. Notifications and Activity Data

We store notification preferences, collection-alert activity, in-app notifications, reminder history, and email notification logs in order to deliver alerts, avoid duplicate messages, and support product operations.

8. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • operate and maintain Whisper
  • verify user eligibility
  • authenticate access to accounts
  • deliver transactional and optional notifications
  • enable opportunity discovery and matching
  • maintain security and detect misuse
  • investigate potential violations of our Terms
  • comply with legal obligations
  • improve product reliability and performance

9. How Information May Be Shared

We may share information with service providers who help operate the platform, such as hosting providers, infrastructure vendors, email delivery services, or verification partners.

We may also disclose information:

  • when required by law
  • to protect the rights, safety, or property of Whisper or its users
  • to investigate misuse or fraud
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or business transfer

10. Data Retention

We retain account, authentication, legal acceptance, certification, notification, and audit records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce our Terms, and comply with legal obligations.

Certain short-lived records, such as temporary authentication tokens, may be deleted automatically after shorter retention periods.

11. Your Choices

You may update certain account details and optional notification preferences within the product.

Transactional communications related to account security, authentication, or system operation may remain enabled because they are necessary to maintain account integrity.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information stored within Whisper. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes occur, the effective date will be updated.

Continued use of Whisper after an updated policy becomes effective means the updated version will apply going forward.
